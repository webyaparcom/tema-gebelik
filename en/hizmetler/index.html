Pregnancy & birth

How do we support you?

Information, preparation and postnatal support at every stage of pregnancy.

  • Pregnancy follow-up

    Regular check-up appointments with a doctor and midwife.

  • Birth preparation class

    The birth process, breathing and positions; partners can join.

  • Breastfeeding support

    One-to-one help with technique and the first weeks.

  • Prenatal pilates & breathing

    Gentle exercise and breathing work with your doctor’s approval.

  • Baby care workshop

    Bathing, sleep routines and everyday care, hands-on.

  • Postnatal support

    Check-ins and counselling during the postnatal weeks.
